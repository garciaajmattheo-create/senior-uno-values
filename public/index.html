How it works

Every subject teacher has a say in who earns the Conduct Award.

01

Advisers upload the class list

Paste names, upload an Excel or CSV file, import the SF1, or add students one by one.

02

Teachers join with a code

Each adviser shares a section access code. Teachers enter it once per term to link the section.

03

Rate the four core values

Maka-Diyos, Makatao, Makakalikasan and Makabansa, marked AO, SO, RO or NO. Individual votes stay anonymous.

04

The awardee rises to the top

Live averages per term, a final mark across three terms, and a tie-break vote if needed.

Official marking

Observed Values, the DepEd way

Marks follow DepEd Order No. 8, s. 2015, Table 13. Averages are always shown back as an official mark.
